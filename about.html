About

Light is the mother of colour — and colour governs my world. Spaces hold infinite possibility — they’re a blank canvas. Put your hands together and endless stories can be told; for me, immersion begins in the details.

I’m Maria — an artist and technician working at the intersection of light, space, and immersive experience. I’m based in Berlin, where I moved in August 2026 to be closer to the work I want to make and the people making it.

My background spans theatre production, festival work, live experience, and immersive environments. I’ve worked as a house lighting technician at one of Ireland’s leading venues, designed for theatre showcases, and collaborated on site-specific light installations. My technical toolkit includes QLab, ETC Eos, MadMapper, and a little TouchDesigner — and I’m actively building depth at the edge where technical operation becomes creative authorship.

Before theatre, I worked across graphic design, visual merchandising, interior design, event coordination, video games, and brand activation. That winding path wasn’t a detour — it built an instinct for space, atmosphere, and audience that I carry into everything I make.

I’m drawn to artists like Olafur Eliasson, Es Devlin, Fujiko Nakaya, and Christopher Bauder — and to environments like teamLab, Meow Wolf, Punchdrunk, WHITEvoid / DARK MATTER, The Dark Rooms, and UNRUHE — because they treat space as a medium and the audience as a participant. My own work lives in that territory. Meeting the Abyss, my first original immersive piece, is in development — a collaboration between human presence and AI that explores what it means to encounter something that isn’t quite like you.

I speak three languages fluently and understand two more. I hold a First Class BA in Marketing and Event Management and a BTEC HND in Technical Theatre with Distinction.

Open to work in immersive spaces, technical theatre, production, events, and festivals — or anywhere that requires a keen eye, a steady hand, and a bit of colour.
